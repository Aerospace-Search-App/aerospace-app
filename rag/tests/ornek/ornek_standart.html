ORNEK-STD-001 Rev. A Structural Design Requirements

1 Scope

This standard establishes structural design requirements for launch vehicle hardware.

2 Applicable Documents

The documents listed in this clause are applicable to the extent specified herein.

3 Definitions

Ultimate load is the limit load multiplied by the ultimate factor of safety.

4 Requirements

4.1 Factors of Safety

Metallic structures shall have a minimum ultimate factor of safety of 1.4.

The following fastener table applies:

3 Bolts M6 torque 12 Nm

Composite structures shall use a factor of 1.5 unless approved by the project.

4.2 Pressure Vessels

Pressure vessels shall be proof tested to 1.5 times maximum expected operating pres-

sure before flight.

4.2.1 Leak Testing

Each vessel shall be leak tested after proof testing.

Annex A Test Levels

A.1 Random Vibration

Qualification random vibration levels shall be 3 dB above acceptance levels.
